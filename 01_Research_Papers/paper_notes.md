# Research Paper Notes

## Purpose

This folder supports Chapter 2 of the research proposal. The proposal states that **43 papers** from Assignment 1 were reviewed to identify common information-security risk themes, existing approaches, limitations, and the research .

The original proposal literature table should remain the primary source for the full paper-by-paper details.

## Main Findings from the 43 Reviewed Studies

The literature review identifies several recurring issues:

1. Manual risk assessment and dependence on expert judgement.
2. Subjective or inconsistent risk scoring.
3. Dependence on predefined risk values.
4. Limited automation and practical tool support.
5. Limited practical testing.

The reviewed studies also use different approaches, including:

- ISO/IEC 27005
- ISO/IEC 27001
- NIST
- FMEA
- AHP
- Risk matrices
- Other structured risk-management and threat-analysis approaches

## Relevance to CRAT

The findings support the need for a simple and measurable prototype that can:

- Accept structured risk inputs.
- Calculate a numerical risk score.
- Classify risk levels consistently.
- Prioritize multiple risks.
- Be tested against a manual baseline.
